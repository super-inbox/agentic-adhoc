# Logo_identity — Travel & Hotel

Six manually inspected, non-duplicate public contest samples selected from the
99designs Travel & Hotel / Logo & Identity listing.

## Folder hierarchy

```text
Logo_identity/
├── Logo Design/
│   └── <contest title>/
├── Logo & Brand Guide/
│   └── <contest title>/
└── Brand Launch Pack/
    └── <contest title>/
```

Each contest directory contains one Winner preview, four public non-winning
previews, a structured `contest-<id>.json` record and a README.

## Coverage

| Subcategory | Contests | Previews |
|---|---:|---:|
| Logo Design | 4 | 20 |
| Logo & Brand Guide | 1 | 5 |
| Brand Launch Pack | 1 | 5 |
| **Total** | **6** | **30** |

This is a top-level collection alongside `Art Design/` and `Travel Hotel/`.
The six contest IDs are distinct from the ten cases stored under
`Travel Hotel/`.

## Evaluation and rights

Non-winning previews mean only "not selected"; they are not objective
low-quality labels. Public Briefs may be incomplete and referenced attachments
are not included. Every contest record retains `training_use: not_cleared` and
`redistribution: not_cleared` pending a documented rights review.
