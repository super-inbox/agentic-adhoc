# Image generation prompts｜v0 territory moodboards

Tool mode: built-in `image_gen`. All three outputs are context-only background plates. The product was intentionally excluded so that its exact identity cannot drift.

## Territory A｜Urban Pause

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: low-cost creative-territory moodboard, background plates only
Input images: Image 1 is the MORI brand-system reference; use only its restrained palette and calm visual tone.
Primary request: Create a clean 2-by-2 contact sheet of four separate photorealistic everyday urban scene thumbnails for the territory “urban pause / one calm day”. Do not include the speaker or any electronic product.
Scene/backdrop, one distinct photo per cell:
1) a simple apartment entry console beside a window at early morning, with a plain canvas commuter tote and keys;
2) a pale stone ledge near an unbranded city transit entrance, soft morning overcast, a plain tote nearby;
3) a quiet pocket-park bench beside an office district at midday, restrained leafy shadow;
4) a calm public riverside bench at dusk, soft city horizon without landmarks.
Style/medium: believable editorial lifestyle photography, accessible real locations, minimal props, low-production-cost staging.
Composition/framing: four equal rectangular photo cells with thin warm-ivory gutters; each scene has a clear empty surface or focal zone where a small cylindrical speaker could later be composited; generous negative space; clear at thumbnail size.
Lighting/mood: restrained natural daylight shifting gently from morning to dusk, calm and unforced.
Color palette: MORI pale blue, warm ivory, muted olive, charcoal; at most a tiny terracotta accent. Keep blue-green open as a future product accent.
Materials/textures: real stone, canvas, pale wood, modest urban greenery.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no logos, no brand marks, no speaker, no electronics, no imaginary accessories, no people, no gold, no glossy luxury styling, no dramatic adventure, no watermark.
```
