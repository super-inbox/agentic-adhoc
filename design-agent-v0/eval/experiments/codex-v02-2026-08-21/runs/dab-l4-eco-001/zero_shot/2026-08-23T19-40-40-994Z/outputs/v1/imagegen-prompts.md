# v1 场景母图生成记录

**模式：** imagegen 内置工具；三次独立生成。  
**用途：** 只生成无产品、无文字的背景母图；产品随后由原始主图确定性合成。

## 1. Urban entry

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: square ecommerce and social campaign background plate for later exact product compositing
Primary request: Create a premium minimalist city-commute entryway scene with NO product in it.
Scene/backdrop: restrained modern apartment entryway, pale cool-gray stone console or bench, one matte charcoal commuter backpack placed softly at the far right rear, subtle urban doorway geometry, abundant uncluttered off-white wall and negative space.
Composition/framing: square editorial product-photography framing; large clean empty placement area on the lower center-right surface for a small cylindrical speaker to be composited later; generous negative space in the upper-left; camera at tabletop height, realistic perspective.
Lighting/mood: controlled cool-neutral daylight from one side, crisp but soft-edged shadow, quiet premium mood, high-end but believable, no dramatic rays.
Color palette: off-white, cool gray, graphite, muted stone only.
Materials/textures: fine plaster, honed stone, matte technical fabric; realistic subtle texture.
Constraints: background plate only; no speaker, no electronics, no product-like cylinder, no text, no letters, no logo, no people, no visible brand, no fictional accessories.
Avoid: gold, yellow, amber, orange, warm wood, brass, ornate decor, busy props, luxury clichés, excessive contrast, oversaturated color, watermark.
```
