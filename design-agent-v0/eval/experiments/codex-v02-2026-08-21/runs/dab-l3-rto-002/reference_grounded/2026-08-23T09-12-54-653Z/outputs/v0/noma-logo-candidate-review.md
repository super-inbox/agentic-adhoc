# NOMA logo candidate review — v0

## Recommendation

Advance **D** to one refinement round, subject to client approval. Reject **A, B, and C** from this route because each relies on a category shorthand the brief explicitly asks NOMA to avoid.

This is a visual review of the supplied candidate board, not a trademark or competitive-landscape clearance.

## Candidate findings

### A — reject: generic house cliché

- The roof outline communicates “neighborhood/home,” but does so through the exact generic-house shorthand the brief excludes.
- The N, O, and roof compete at 24 px; the mark remains visible, but its crowded overlap weakens immediate NOMA recognition.
- The N/O construction provides some brand specificity, which is why A ranks above C on ownability, but the familiar house frame creates elevated look-alike risk.

### B — reject: smile-app and bowl cliché

- It has a friendly tone and survives reduction well, but the fast read is a smiley/app badge or bowl rather than NOMA.
- The circular container, face-like arcs, dot, and bowl are all familiar icon ingredients. Their combination is especially close to the two directions the brief asks to avoid: smile-app and generic food icon.
- Strong icon recognition is not the same as strong brand recognition; this candidate demonstrates that distinction.

### C — reject: literal noodle/food cliché

- The warm linework feels handcrafted and food-related, which gives it some brief fit at display size.
- Its premise still reads as steam/noodle flowing into a bowl or plated form, directly conflicting with the instruction to avoid literal noodle clichés.
- At 24 px the thin bends and multiple internal turns are less immediate than the other candidates. The added visual detail does not make the underlying category idea more original or more ownable.

### D — advance: strongest candidate

- The N-plus-arch structure is bold, contemporary, and the most NOMA-specific silhouette in the set.
- The terracotta arch supplies warmth, while the abstract construction avoids making food the literal subject.
- It is the clearest symbol at 24 px and has the best path to a durable symbol-plus-wordmark system.
- Risk is not zero: the arch may drift toward doorway, architecture, or property-brand language. That is a refinement issue, not grounds for rejecting the route at this stage.

## One-round refinement brief for D

Keep the core N-plus-arch concept and do not reopen exploration.

1. Tune the black N/terracotta arch junctions and stroke relationships so the construction feels intentional in both color and one color.
2. Preserve the open counters and distinct N silhouette at 24 px; verify symbol-only, reversed, and monochrome exports from vector artwork.
3. Adjust the arch proportions only as needed to reduce a generic doorway/property read while retaining warmth and the current bold silhouette.
4. Rebalance wordmark weight and spacing against the symbol so neither component dominates.

## 24 px test note

The evidence crops normalize each supplied symbol to a 24 × 24 px raster tile. Because the source is a presentation-board raster rather than production vector artwork, the result is directional; final pixel fit should be checked from the vector master.

No fifth design was created, and none of the four candidates was altered.
