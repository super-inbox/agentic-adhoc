# ImageGen prompt set · v0

Mode: built-in ImageGen. The product image was **not** regenerated or edited by ImageGen. ImageGen supplied three background plates only; the exact supplied product photo was then placed deterministically.

## Direction A background

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: background plate for a low-cost perfume key-visual concept exploration, 4:5 portrait
Primary request: Create a quiet tactile nocturnal background only, with no product and no text, suitable for placing an exact existing perfume photograph on top later.
Scene/backdrop: layered warm off-white handmade paper and fine linen, a single very soft circular pool of dim evening light, sparse terracotta-red edge accent, generous calm negative space.
Style/medium: premium editorial still-life photography, restrained and achievable in a small tabletop studio.
Composition/framing: 4:5 portrait; broad uninterrupted central and lower negative space; subtle asymmetry; no frame or collage panels.
Lighting/mood: low-contrast warm night ambience, quiet, low stimulation, tactile bedtime ritual; not dreamy, mystical, clinical, or medical.
Color palette: warm off-white, charcoal-black shadow, muted terracotta red; no blue, purple, neon, or saturated colors.
Materials/textures: visible paper tooth and linen weave, matte surfaces, soft natural falloff.
Text: none.
Constraints: background only; no bottles, labels, logos, words, people, beds, pills, candles, moons, stars, clocks, smoke, flowers, or therapeutic symbolism.
Avoid: glossy luxury cliches, spa imagery, medical advertising, sleep claims, magical glow, excessive props, harsh contrast, watermark.
```
