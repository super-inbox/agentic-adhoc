## A1 — Clay Horizon

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: low-cost key-visual background concept, 4:5 portrait
Input images: Image 1 is a brand-system reference only; use its cream, terracotta, pale blue, olive, charcoal palette and calm tactile sensibility. Do not reproduce its logo, words, typography specimens, or board layout.
Primary request: TERRITORY A — STILL HORIZON, concept A1 “Clay Horizon.” Create a minimal studio set that can be built cheaply with painted paper and one light: warm cream handmade-paper wall and floor, a low terracotta-red horizon plane occupying roughly the bottom quarter, and one very soft pale-blue dusk glow grazing the wall.
Composition/framing: straight-on, spacious 4:5 vertical composition; keep a quiet open focal zone through the center and lower center for a product packshot to be composited later; restrained geometry.
Lighting/mood: low-contrast evening light, soft shadow falloff, silent and tactile, nighttime ritual without sleep or medical cues.
Materials/textures: subtle paper fibers and matte mineral pigment, no glossy effects.
Constraints: background only; no bottle, no product, no people, no props, no logo, no leaf mark, no text, no letters, no watermark. Preserve the brand palette with cream dominant and terracotta clearly present. Avoid bedrooms, beds, pills, moons, stars, clinical lighting, wellness clichés, smoke, incense, and efficacy symbolism.
```
