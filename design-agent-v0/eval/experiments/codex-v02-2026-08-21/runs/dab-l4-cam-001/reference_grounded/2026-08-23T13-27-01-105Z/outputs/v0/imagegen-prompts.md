## Territory A background

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: landscape key-visual exploration background plate, no product and no copy  
Primary request: create one refined abstract studio background for a calm nighttime fragrance campaign territory called Quiet Geometry, using only low-cost graphic means that could be reproduced with paper, color blocks, and soft shadow.  
Input image: the attached MORI brand board is a palette and brand-token reference only; do not recreate or alter the logo.  
Scene/backdrop: warm rice-white ground with one restrained terracotta-red horizontal plane, a narrow charcoal accent, and a very subtle muted olive paper edge.  
Style/medium: premium editorial still-life background, tactile matte paper, minimalist Japanese-Scandinavian restraint, photoreal surface texture.  
Composition/framing: 3:2 landscape, generous open central and upper-left areas, no objects, no bottle, no packaging, no typography.  
Lighting/mood: very soft directional evening light, slow and quiet, low contrast, gentle paper-cast shadows.  
Color palette: preserve the brand board's rice-white, terracotta red, charcoal, muted olive; no saturated colors.  
Materials/textures: uncoated paper, faint fibers, subtle deckled edge.  
Constraints: background only; no product; no logo; no words; no letters; no watermark; no medical symbols; no moon, stars, bed, pills, clinical cues, or sleep claims.
