# Grain & Glow — Territory B v1 delta refinement

## Client decision recorded

- Territory A is rejected as still too influencer/template-like.
- Territory C is rejected as too old.
- Territory B, `酥层见光 / Layers in Light`, is selected for refinement.
- Requested delta: more whitespace, no Logo change, and a more expensive-looking gift box.

## Changed in v1

1. **More whitespace.** The visual moves from a dark, close macro filling most of the frame to an expansive warm-stone field. The box sits in the lower-right and leaves roughly three-fifths of the composition calm.
2. **More expensive gift-box cues.** The concept uses a dense rigid-box proportion, fine uncoated paper wrap, crisp beveled edges, a blind-deboss perimeter, a deep-brown base, and one very thin metallic edge. Price perception comes from restraint and material fidelity rather than more decoration.
3. **Logo placed from the locked source.** The generated box was intentionally blank. The visible current Logo was then extracted from the v0 locked crop and placed through one planar perspective transform. It was not regenerated, redrawn, recolored, split, or replaced.

## Preserved from approved Territory B

- `层／脆／光` remains the primary recognition idea.
- The v0 Territory B palette tokens remain unchanged: `#1D110C`, `#A9531F`, `#E5A03C`, `#F1D5A2`. Only their area ratio changes, with more warm-stone space.
- A single flaky-pastry cue remains the craft evidence.
- No influencer props, café scene, flowers, tea set, sticker collage, new pattern, or new decorative motif was added.
- All v0 artifacts remain unchanged under `outputs/v0`.
