# NOMA logo vote

## Winner: D

Option D is the strongest overall. Its arch-and-**N** monogram feels distinctive, confident, and contemporary; it also stays recognizable at small sizes and works naturally in one color. The symbol and wordmark have the best visual balance of the four.

### Ranking

1. **D** — most memorable, polished, and scalable.
2. **A** — communicates “home,” but the overlapping roof, N, and O feel busy.
3. **B** — friendly and readable, though the bowl/face motif is fairly generic and strongly limits the brand to food.
4. **C** — warm and artisanal, but too illustrative and ambiguous for a versatile primary logo.

If NOMA is specifically a restaurant, B becomes a stronger category cue; for the best all-purpose brand identity, D remains the clear choice.
