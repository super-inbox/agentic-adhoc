## The real reference finding

Curify consumes references on **14/21**, not 0/21. The 7 failures are exactly
the 7 cases whose own export records `omitted_asset_roles`:

    AR-007  AR-008  AR-009  TIQ-088  TIQ-096  TIQ-098  TIQ-100

and the dropped roles are **secondary, typed** references —
`garment_reference` (AR-007/8/9, AR-009 drops two) and `product_reference`
(TIQ-088/96/98/100). The primary `edit_target` loads every time.

So this is not "references don't work". It is **multi-reference / typed-slot
references are silently dropped**, which is the defect the multi-reference input
fix targets, and it is worth 7 cases of hard-gate failure.

  means are over n=19 — mildly favourable to Codex.

- The published Curify export has no `states`/`trajectory`, so
  `target_stage_coverage` and tool-call counts are **unmeasurable, not zero**.
